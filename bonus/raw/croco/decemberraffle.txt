## ABOUT THE PROMOTION
Sports Raffle  - a promotion where you collect tickets in the corresponding raffle drums by making deposits and placing bets on any sport. If your ticket is drawn, you win prizes: a Mini Cooper Countryman C, cash prizes, an iPhone, and a PlayStation. 
  Participating products: Sports and Deposit  
 Minimum number of selections in a sports bet slip - 2, minimum total odds - 1.5; system bets and bets placed on Golden Race do not qualify;

You can collect tickets daily - from November 24 at 00:00 to December 30 at 23:59. 
   
 You have a 5-level progress that you complete with your total deposits made during the day: 
  100₾ (+1 ticket) > 250₾ (+2 tickets), 500₾ (+5 tickets), 1,000₾ (+10 tickets), 2,500₾ (+20 tickets).  
   
 You can collect a maximum of 38 tickets per day. 
   
 100 winners will be announced daily, and the prizes will be distributed as follows: 
   
 Winner 1 - iPhone 18 Pro Max Winner 2 - iPhone 18 Pro Winner 3 - PlayStation Slim Winner 4 - 1 000₾ Winner 5 - 900₾ Winner 6 - 800₾ Winner 7 - 700₾ Winner 8 - 600₾ Winner 9 - 500₾ Winner 10/11 - 400₾ Winner 12/13 - 300₾ Winner 14/16 - 250₾ Winner 17/19 - 200₾ Winner 20/23 - 150₾ Winner 24/60 - 100₾ Winner 61/100 - 50₾   
 The raffle will be held daily at 14:00 (e.g., tickets collected in the November 24 raffle drum will be drawn on November 25 at 14:00, and so on). 
   
 * Cash prizes will be credited by 16:00 the following day, while if you win an electronics, you will receive an SMS voucher (by16:00 the following day), which you can use on Croco Shop only to purchase the prize you won. 
 * The raffle will be conducted entirely online. Winners will be selected at random and announced online;
## SPORTS RAFFLE - TICKET ACCUMULATION AND PRIZES
The Sports Raffle consists of 5 stages, and tickets for each stage can be collected on the following dates: 
   
  Stage I  - November 24, 00:00 – November 30, 23:59 / The raffle will be held on December 1 at 16:00. 
  Stage II  - December 1, 00:00 – December 7, 23:59 / The raffle will be held on December 8 at 16:00. 
  Stage III  - December 8, 00:00 – December 14, 23:59 / The raffle will be held on December 15 at 16:00. 
  Stage IV  - December 15, 00:00 – December 21, 23:59 / The raffle will be held on December 22 at 16:00. 
  Stage V  - December 22, 00:00 – December 30, 23:59 / The raffle will be held on December 31 at 15:00. 
   
 To collect tickets, you have a betting progress system that you can complete with your total sports bets placed during the day: 
  500₾ (+1 ticket), 1,000₾ (+2 tickets), 2,500₾ (+5 tickets), 5,000₾ (+10 tickets), 10,000₾ (+20 tickets).  
   
 You can collect a maximum of 38 tickets per day. 
   
 10 winners will be announced in each stage of the raffle, and the prizes will be distributed as follows: 
   
 Winner 1 - Mini Cooper Winner 2 - 25 000₾ Winner 3/4 - 15 000₾  Winner 5/6 - 10 000₾  Winner 7 - 8 000₾  Winner 8 - 7 000₾  Winner 9/10 - 5 000₾    
 * Cash prizes will be credited by 16:00 the following day, while if you win a car, a company representative will contact you. 
 * The car visuals used in promotional materials may not accurately reflect the actual vehicle; 
 * The raffle is held entirely online, meaning that winners will be determined online at random; 
 * The Mini Cooper color for Stage I and II raffles is Slate Blue, for Stage III - Smokey Green, and for Stages IV and V - Melting Silver.
